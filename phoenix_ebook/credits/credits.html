The Phoenix Library Service

This digital book
was produced in connection with the Phoenix Library Service
a project of Flaming Hydra

Libraries can buy, download, store, and loan our books for keeps, with no licensing restrictions; intellectual property rights remain with the author, just as with a traditional paper library book. Our service aims to preserve the traditional terms and spirit of the pre-internet relationship between authors, publishers, and libraries.

For more on our programs and publications, please visit flaminghydra.com.
